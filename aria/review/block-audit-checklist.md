# Block Audit Checklist

- Confirm active block and commit being audited.
- Confirm source of truth/context files read.
- Confirm AGENTS.md reading order was followed.
- Confirm scope boundaries.
- Confirm non-goals.
- Confirm changed files are expected.
- Confirm validation was run.
- Confirm repository hygiene.
- Confirm no unrelated refactors.
- Confirm no invented business rules.
- Confirm no private/local artifacts.
- Confirm final report format.
